# Changelog

All notable changes to StormShelter are documented here.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-08

First release.

### Added
- Physics-based shelter building with straw, wood, brick and stone pieces
  (blocks, posts, planks, long planks and roof pieces).
- 99-second construction countdown with a weather warning; storm starts when
  time runs out or when the player presses **Done!**.
- Storm phase: the shelter must keep the kids safe and dry for 5 seconds.
- 100 levels across five storm types: High Winds, Gale, Thunderstorm,
  Tornado (wind changes direction) and Hurricane.
- Easy, Medium and Hard difficulties.
- 3 save slots, automatic progress saving, and in-game Save Game with resume.
- For Parents section: progress, score, stars, play time, recent attempts and
  the mistakes made, with tips.
- Procedural ambient background music with an On/Off toggle; procedural sound
  effects.
- Credits screen with the GPL-3.0 license text.
- Linux `.deb` package and Windows installer and portable `.exe` builds.
